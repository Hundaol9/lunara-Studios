ABOUT US

We explore ideas through image, movement, sound, and story.

Our Story

Founded in 1996, Lunara Studios is the next phase for founder Brent Kirsten. After establishing Digitalcore as one of San Francisco’s longest-running post-production facilities, Brent has now launched Lunara as a full-service creative company.




At Lunara, creativity is about more than simply making things look good — it’s about creating visuals that feel memorable, unexpected, and alive. The studio brings together different creative perspectives to turn ideas into experiences that connect with audiences.

Our Approach

Every project begins with an idea. We take the time to understand it, explore its possibilities, and shape it into something with movement, character, and purpose.

01

Discover

We begin by understanding the idea, its purpose, audience, and the story it needs to tell.

Explore

We turn the strongest ideas into animation and visual experiences that bring the original vision to life.

What Guides Us

Creativity

We approach every project with curiosity and an open mind, exploring ideas from different perspectives to discover fresh and unexpected possibilities. From the first concept to the final frame, we experiment with movement, color, sound, and visual storytelling to create experiences that are engaging, expressive, and meaningful.

Collaboration

We believe the strongest creative work comes from bringing different perspectives together. We work closely with clients, directors, designers, and creative teams throughout every stage of a project, sharing ideas, listening carefully, and building on each other’s strengths to turn a vision into something clear, cohesive, and memorable.

Originality

We believe every project deserves its own identity. Rather than relying on familiar formulas or trends, we look for ideas that feel specific to the story, brand, and audience. We develop distinctive visual languages and creative approaches that give each project its own character and make the final result feel genuinely unique.

At Lunara, every project starts with an idea and grows through creativity, movement, and collaboration. We turn concepts into bold visual experiences that connect, engage, and stay with you.
