Our Story

Founded in 1996, Lunara Studios is the next phase for founder Brent Kirsten. After establishing Digitalcore as one of San Francisco’s longest-running post-production facilities, Brent has now launched Lunara as a full-service creative company.




At Lunara, creativity is about more than simply making things look good — it’s about creating visuals that feel memorable, unexpected, and alive. The studio brings together different creative perspectives to turn ideas into experiences that connect with audiences.
